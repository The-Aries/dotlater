A simple browser tab reminder

One dot. One click.
Come back later.

Keep a tab in sight without keeping it in mind. Mark it yellow and let DotLater clear the mark when you return.

Get started View releases

Get started in three steps

1

Pin DotLater

Open the browser's Extensions menu (puzzle-piece icon) and choose Pin or Show in toolbar. In Safari, enable the extension in Safari Settings first.

2

Mark any tab

Click the green dot. It turns yellow to mark the tab. Click it again to unmark.

3

Return when ready

Switch to another tab. When you return, the mark clears after one second by default. You can change the delay in extension settings.

Useful to know

Keyboard shortcut

Press Ctrl + Shift + X on Windows/Linux or ⌘ + Shift + X on Mac to toggle the mark.

Change the delay

Open DotLater → Extension options in your browser's extension manager. Settings are stored by your browser, not this website.

Some tabs look different

Browser-protected pages might not allow marking. Safari uses a yellow prefix in the page title when its favicon cannot update.
