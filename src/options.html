DotLater

Get started

  1. Find DotLater in your browser's Extensions menu (puzzle-piece icon). Choose Pin or Show in toolbar to keep the green dot visible. In Safari, enable the extension in Safari Settings first.
  2. On a regular web page, click the green dot to mark that tab for later. The dot turns yellow. Click again to undo.
  3. Switch to another tab. When you return to the marked tab, the mark clears automatically after the delay below.

Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + X (Windows/Linux) or Command + Shift + X (Mac).

Settings

The countdown starts only after you switch away from a marked tab and come back. Switching away before the timer expires preserves the mark. A change to this setting takes effect the next time you mark a tab.
